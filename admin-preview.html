Lead table columns

Which columns affiliates can see, chosen per comp model.

The registry is a hard constraint, not a default. A column that does not list a comp model cannot be enabled for it at all — that is why the revenue rows below have no toggle on the CPL side. Locked columns cannot be switched off.

Columns forbidden to every comp model

Absent from the registry entirely. There is no toggle, and there must never be one.

The July 31 Heritage leak was Profit, Buyer Name, CSR Name and Call Result. Those stay forbidden to everyone, revenue share included.

Spend & volume targets

A revenue-share payout target is measured on the sold date; CPL spend stays on the received date. Getting this backwards reports $0 for the first ten days of every month.
