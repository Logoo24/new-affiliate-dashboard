Spend & volume targets — CPL only

Weekly. Set margin or CPL, then volume or spend — the paired field fills in.

Margin and CPL are the same lever, twice: CPL = R × (1 − margin), where R is our trailing revenue per accepted lead — internal only, never shown to the affiliate. Volume and spend are the second pair: Spend = Volume × CPL. Volume is defined in accepted leads, matching how CPL is invoiced. The daily figure on the affiliate's chart is this weekly volume × a day-of-week split that defaults to Sunday 0% and is overridable per partner — see ADMIN-MAPPING.md §4.
